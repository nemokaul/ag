Chapter 14 RAG for Video Content Generation and Stock Production

The application of retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) to video content creation represents a revolutionary convergence of artificial intelligence (AI) and multimedia production. As the demand for video content skyrockets across social media, corporate communications, education, and entertainment, traditional production methods struggle to keep pace with both volume and quality expectations.
